About Us

Balochistan's Most Trusted Real Estate Platform

Quetta Digital Property (QDP.pk) was founded in 2023 with a simple mission: to make real estate transactions in Balochistan transparent, accessible, and hassle-free. We noticed that the property market in the province was largely unorganized, with buyers and sellers relying on word-of-mouth and local brokers.

Our platform brings together verified properties, trusted agents, and comprehensive market information under one roof. Whether you're looking for a house in Quetta, a plot in Gwadar, or an investment opportunity in Turbat, QDP.pk is your one-stop destination.

We currently cover 7 districts across Balochistan with over 2,500 listed properties and 120+ verified real estate agents. Our goal is to expand to every major city in the province by 2026.
